# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Borrowers in India seeking personal, business, home, gold, and vehicle financing. Users range from first-time applicants needing transparent guidance on interest rates (reducing vs flat APR) to experienced entrepreneurs seeking working capital and rapid collateral-free disbursal.

## Product Purpose
Loan Aasra is a transparent, empowering loan advisory and comparison platform. It simplifies complex lending decisions, demystifies real borrowing costs, and enables borrowers to calculate and compare real repayment costs before committing.

## Positioning
"Your Dreams, Our Support." Unlike aggressive aggregators that obscure loan charges and trigger immediate spam calls, Loan Aasra prioritizes borrower financial clarity, transparent APR terms, and instant interactive calculations.

## Operating Context
Mobile and desktop responsive web, loan budgeting, pre-qualification checks, instant inquiry dispatch, and seamless multilingual advisory in English, Hindi, and Punjabi across `loanaasra.com`.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Real-time interactive EMI calculation with dynamic ₹ INR formatting.
- 13 comprehensive loan categories with transparent rate comparisons.
- Full multilingual accessibility: English (en), Hindi (hi), and Punjabi (pa).
- Lead inquiry capture modal with zero-friction fields.
- Detailed borrower educational repository explaining flat vs reducing rates, debt consolidation, and credit scores.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Loan Aasra
- Tagline: Your Dreams, Our Support. - Compare, Calculate & Apply
- Domain: `https://loanaasra.com`
- Core Palette: Deep Navy (`#0A1C3E` / `#0A2540`), Radiant Amber-Gold (`#F59E0B` / `#DCA035`), Cobalt Interactive (`#0052FF`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- Primary Typography: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Outfit`, `Syne` (with `Mukta` for Devanagari/Hindi and `Mukta Mahee` for Gurmukhi/Punjabi).
- Voice: Reassuring, authoritative, transparent, and direct.

## Product Principles
1. **Clarity Over Complexity**: Demystify true APR and total interest before asking for personal details.
2. **Calculate Before You Commit**: Interactive calculator snapshots serve as the primary user anchor.
3. **Transparent Advisory**: Educate borrowers on loan mechanics to prevent high-cost debt traps.
4. **Inclusive Accessibility**: Deliver native linguistic fluency and cultural resonance in English, Hindi, and Punjabi without condescending oversimplification.
