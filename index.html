Common questions

Clear answers, before you apply

Every lender has its own policies. Always review the latest offer documents and ask for clarification on anything you do not understand.

More questions? Contact us
How do I estimate my monthly EMI?

Enter a loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure in the EMI calculator. The result is an estimate based on a standard reducing-balance calculation and may differ from a lender's final schedule.

What affects loan eligibility?

Lenders may assess income, employment or business stability, credit history, existing monthly obligations, requested amount, tenure and their own lending policies.

Does checking eligibility guarantee approval?

No. An eligibility check is only an initial indication, if offered. Final approval, interest rate and terms depend on lender verification and underwriting.

What charges should I check besides interest?

Review processing fees, applicable taxes, late-payment charges, prepayment or foreclosure terms, insurance if applicable, and the total annualised cost of borrowing.

Can I repay a loan early?

It depends on the lender, loan type and agreement. Check whether part-prepayment or foreclosure is permitted and whether any charges or conditions apply.

Are investment returns guaranteed?

Not always. Mutual funds and many other investments carry market risk. Read the product documents and understand the risks, fees and withdrawal conditions before investing.
