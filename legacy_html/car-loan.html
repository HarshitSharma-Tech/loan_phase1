Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions about Car Loan

Answers to standard queries to help you borrow with confidence.

What does 100% on-road financing mean?

On-road financing covers the car's ex-showroom price plus state road tax, RTO registration charges, and insurance, requiring minimal to zero out-of-pocket cash at delivery.

Can I buy a used or pre-owned car with Finora?

Yes. We finance certified used cars up to 10 years of total age with loans up to 85% of evaluated value.

Can I pay off my car loan early?

Yes, you can prepay or foreclose your vehicle loan after 6 monthly installments by paying nominal foreclosure fees.
