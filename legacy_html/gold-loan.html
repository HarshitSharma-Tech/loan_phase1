Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions about Gold Loan

Answers to standard queries to help you borrow with confidence.

Is my gold safe with the bank?

Yes. Your gold is weighed, purity-checked in your presence, sealed in a tamper-proof bag with your signature, and stored in an RBI-compliant high-security bank vault with comprehensive insurance.

What happens if gold prices drop after taking the loan?

Lenders maintain a 25% safety margin under the 75% LTV rule. If prices drop drastically, you may be requested to deposit additional gold or make a small margin payment.

What is bullet repayment in a gold loan?

In bullet repayment, you do not pay any monthly principal or interest during the tenure. You pay the accumulated interest and full principal in one go at the end of the loan term.
