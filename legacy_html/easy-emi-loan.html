Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions about Easy EMI Loan

Answers to standard queries to help you borrow with confidence.

How does a No-Cost EMI work?

In a No-Cost EMI, the merchant or brand provides an upfront discount equivalent to the total interest charge, so you pay exactly the product price split across monthly installments with zero extra interest.

Do I need a credit card to use Easy EMI?

No! Finora Easy EMI works with your regular savings bank account via UPI autopay or debit card mandate.

Can I buy multiple items under my approved limit?

Yes! Your approved limit can be used across multiple purchases as long as your total outstanding remains within your sanctioned ceiling.
