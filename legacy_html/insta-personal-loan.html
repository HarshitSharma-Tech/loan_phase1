Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions about Insta Personal Loan

Answers to standard queries to help you borrow with confidence.

How is an Insta Personal Loan different from a regular Personal Loan?

An Insta Personal Loan is 100% automated and paperless with pre-approved underwriting, allowing disbursals in under 15 minutes without visiting any branch.

Do I need to submit physical paper documents?

No. The entire verification takes place via Aadhaar OTP, PAN database check, and automated netbanking account aggregators.

Can I apply if I am not an existing customer?

Yes, new customers with a solid credit score and verifiable income can complete a 3-minute video KYC to receive instant sanction.
