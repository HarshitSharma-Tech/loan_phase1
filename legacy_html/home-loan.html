Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions about Home Loan

Answers to standard queries to help you borrow with confidence.

What are the tax benefits of a Finora home loan?

Under Section 24(b), you can claim up to ₹2,00,000 per financial year against interest paid on a self-occupied home. Under Section 80C, principal repayment up to ₹1,50,000 is deductible.

Can I transfer my existing home loan to Finora?

Yes! Our balance transfer service helps you refinance from high-interest lenders to partner bank rates as low as 8.40% p.a. with minimal legal processing.

What percentage of the property value can I borrow?

As per RBI guidelines: up to 90% for property value up to ₹30 Lakhs; up to 80% for loans between ₹30 Lakhs and ₹75 Lakhs; and up to 75% for loans above ₹75 Lakhs.
